Privacy Policy for Trimbell

Welcome to Trimbell, a salon appointment booking platform designed to connect customers with salons and help salon owners manage their businesses. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect
a. From Customers:
  Name, phone number, and email address
  Appointment details (selected services, time slots)
  Location (if permission is granted)
  Device information and usage data

b. From Salon Owners:
  Business name and contact info
  Salon details (services, timings, photos)
  Booking and customer history

2. How We Use Your Information
To enable appointment booking and scheduling
To display available services and salon details
To send appointment notifications and reminders
To support in-app features and performance
To communicate updates, offers, or important notices

3. Sharing of Information
We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your data only:
With salons you book with (for appointment processing)
With service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics)
If required by law or legal processes

4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. User Controls
You can update or delete your profile data via the app.
You may disable location permissions in your device settings.
You can contact us to request deletion of your account.

6. Children’s Privacy
Trimbell is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app or on our website.

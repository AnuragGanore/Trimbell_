# How to Delete Your Account on Trimbell

At Trimbell, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your account.

If you wish to delete your Trimbell account, please follow these steps:

---

## Steps to Delete Your Account

1. Open the **Trimbell** app on your phone.
2. Go to the **Profile** section.
3. Tap the **Delete Account** button.
4. An alert box will appear asking:  
   _"Are you sure you want to delete your account?"_
5. Tap **Yes** to confirm.

Once confirmed, your account and all associated data (such as profile info, appointment history, etc.) will be **permanently deleted** and cannot be recovered.

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

- Your name, email, and phone number
- Appointment history
- Saved salon preferences
- Any other personal information

No data is retained after account deletion.
